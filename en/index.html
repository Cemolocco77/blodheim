The game

A warrior's saga

Train, gear up, fight: every victory brings you a step closer to Valhalla.

Train your warrior

Train six attributes with gold and gear up nine slots. Six qualities from Common to Divine and hundreds of prefixes and suffixes make every item different. Rise to level 150.

Expeditions and caves

Fight bandits, wolves, giants and dragons across more than 30 regions. Push deeper through 34 caves, build a team with your companions and, if you dare, descend into the Underworld.

Arena

Challenge rivals, earn honour and climb the rankings. Fight shoulder to shoulder with your team in the Cave Arena.

The Smith

Forge items from recipes and melt old ones down into materials. More than 70 forging materials; grow stronger with runes, powders and whetstones.

Clan village

Found your clan and grow its village of 12 buildings: Longhouse, Treasure, Training Ground, Healing House, Temple and more. Challenge rival clans.

Asgard and events

Serve six gods, gather their favour and earn their blessings. Timed events bring more loot and special costumes.

On the way

Online Blodheim

Today Blodheim is played solo: arena rivals, the market and clan members are computer-controlled. In the online version we are working on, you will:

  • Fight characters and teams built by real players in the Arena and the Cave Arena
  • Found a clan with real players and grow your village together
  • Message other players and trade with them at the Market and the Auction House
  • Keep your progress on your account, safe when you change devices

We will announce the release date here.
