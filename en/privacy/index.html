Privacy Policy

Blodheim · Developer: Dengizli Games (Cem Dengizli) · Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the Blodheim mobile game (iOS and Android) and this website use and how. In short: the game does not collect your personal data, does not send it to any server and does not share it with anyone.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID) and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

  • Game save: your character, progress, items and settings are stored only on your own device. Deleting the game deletes the save.
  • Profile picture (optional): if you choose a profile picture from your photo library, only the photo you pick is accessed; it stays on your device and is never uploaded. Photo access is not required and can be revoked at any time in your device settings.
  • Notifications (optional): reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, not sent from a server. You can turn them off in your device settings.

Bug reports and support

The in-game "Report Bug" option opens a draft in your own email app. The draft contains the game version, device model, operating system, screen size, your player name, level, gold and gem amounts, and recent error logs. This information reaches us only if you send the email yourself, and it is used only to fix the problem. Write to us if you want us to delete an email you sent.

Purchases

There are no real-money purchases in the game. Gems and gold are earned in-game only.

Invite code

Your invite code is generated from the save on your device. When you share it, the text goes only to the app you choose or to the clipboard.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Everything, including fonts and images, is loaded from the site itself; no requests are sent to other servers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes (GitHub Privacy Statement).

Children

Because the game collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a feature that uses data is added (for example an online account or cloud saves), this page will be updated in advance and the date will change.
