Report from inside the game

  1. Open Settings in the game and tap Report Bug / Send Suggestion (or the small Report Bug button at the bottom right of the screen).
  2. Briefly describe what happened and tap Send.
  3. Your phone's email app opens a draft with the game version, device model and recent error logs, which help us find the problem. You send the email yourself.

Adding a screenshot helps us find the problem much faster.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my save stored?

In the current version there are no accounts or cloud saves: your character and progress are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes the save. In the online version your progress will be tied to your account.

How do I delete my account and data?

The current version has no accounts and we keep no data about you. Uninstalling the game deletes the whole save. If you have emailed us and want that email deleted, just write to us. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Are there real-money purchases?

No. Gems and gold are earned in-game only.

Are arena rivals and clan members real players?

Not yet; they are computer-controlled. Playing with real players will arrive with the online version.

How do I change the language?

In the game, open Settings > Dil / Language. The game can be played in English and Turkish.

Which devices are supported?

Android 8.0 or later, iOS 15 or later.

How do I turn off notifications?

Turn off the notification permission for Blodheim in your device settings.
